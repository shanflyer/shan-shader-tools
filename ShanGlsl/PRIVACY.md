# Privacy

Shan GLSL has no analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud storage or built-in network service.
It reads shader text and referenced include files locally to provide editing features.
Live diagnostics launch the compiler executable configured by the user. Shader snapshots
are written into a temporary directory, passed to that compiler, and removed after the check.

The behavior of an independently configured compiler is outside this plugin's control.
JetBrains IDE services and Marketplace downloads have their own privacy policies.
